    One system for citizens, responders and managers.

    Each group sees what it needs, from the same live data.

    Citizens

    Report a flood, landslide, fire or blocked road with a photo or video. Find the nearest shelter with space, food, water and medical help, then navigate to it.

    Rescue teams

    See which teams are free, what they carry (boat, ambulance, medical kit) and which incident they are on. Dispatch the closest match in one click.

    Disaster managers

    Watch every incident on a live map, broadcast alerts to nearby users, track water, food and medical stock, and follow missing and rescued people.

    From report to dispatch, all in the cloud.

    Every report follows the same path, so nothing waits in a queue by accident.

    1. API gatewayReceives the report from the app or website.
    2. Serverless functionClassifies the incident and scores its priority from 0 to 100.
    3. Cloud databaseStores the incident, photos and location.
    4. Notification serviceAlerts the right team and nearby residents.
    5. Auto-scalingAdds capacity automatically when reports surge.
